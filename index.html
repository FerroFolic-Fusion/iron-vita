02 — A SMALL HABIT TO KNOW

Give your iron
room to absorb.

Tea and coffee contain compounds called polyphenols that can reduce the absorption of non-haem iron when consumed with meals.

Practical tip
Try having tea or coffee between meals rather than alongside an iron-rich meal. This is a helpful habit, not a reason to avoid these drinks completely.

People with diagnosed iron deficiency or specific medical needs should follow advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

03 — NUTRITION THROUGH LIFE

Extra attention
at important stages.

Nutrition needs can change with age and life stage. A varied diet and appropriate professional guidance both matter.

🤰
MATERNAL NUTRITION

Pregnant mothers

Folate is important for healthy cell division and early fetal development. Adequate iron supports the increased blood supply during pregnancy. Supplement needs should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

🌱
GROWING YEARS

Adolescent girls

Growth and menstrual blood loss can increase iron needs. Regular meals with iron-rich foods, vitamin C sources and good dietary variety can help support nutritional health.

🎒
SCHOOL WELLNESS

School-age children

Nutritious snacks contribute to energy and dietary variety. Homemade laddus made with ingredients such as sesame, roasted gram and nuts can be one option within a balanced diet.
